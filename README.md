## 📌 Project Overview

The **Sister Cargo Barge Project** involved the engineering development and production support of a steel cargo barge based on an existing vessel design.

The engineering scope included the review and development of hull structural and outfitting drawings, material take-off, fabrication requirements, and engineering revisions to support shipyard production and installation activities.

---

## 👨‍💻 My Role

**Naval Architect / Hull Engineer**

My responsibilities covered engineering drawing development, design review, material take-off, outfitting arrangement, and coordination between engineering and production teams.

---

## 🛠️ Key Responsibilities

* Reviewed consultant-issued structural drawings.
* Reviewed and prepared **Material Take-Off (MTO)**.
* Developed and revised hull structural drawings.
* Prepared hull outfitting and deck outfitting drawings.
* Prepared fabrication and installation details.
* Developed details for marine equipment and deck fittings.
* Reviewed dimensions and arrangements for production requirements.
* Revised drawings based on actual fabrication conditions.
* Coordinated engineering requirements with production and QC teams.
* Supported drawing submission and approval requirements.

---

## ⚓ Engineering Scope

### Hull Structure

* Deck structure
* Longitudinal stiffeners
* Transverse frames
* Brackets
* Pillars and supports
* Local reinforcement
* Structural connections
* Fabrication details

### Hull Outfitting

* Bollards
* Fairleads
* Manholes
* Ladders
* Handrails
* Equipment supports
* Access arrangements
